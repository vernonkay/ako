# Contributing to ako

Thanks for your interest. ako is a personal open-source project at the idea stage, and
right now the most valuable contributions are not code, they are thinking.

## How to help today

- **Shape the idea.** Read [VISION.md](VISION.md) and [docs/concept.md](docs/concept.md),
  then come to [Discussions](https://github.com/vernonkay/ako/discussions) with where you
  agree, where you do not, and what we have missed. The open questions in
  [`docs/get-involved.md`](docs/get-involved.md) are the best place to start.
- **Bring domain knowledge.** If you teach machining, run a lathe, run a makerspace, or
  have been hurt or nearly hurt by one, your experience is worth more than any
  architecture diagram here. Safety and curriculum are the parts we most need to get
  right.
- **Bring realtime-AI knowledge.** If you have built with realtime voice-and-vision
  frameworks or on-device perception, tell us where the concept is naive.
- **A concrete proposal or a problem to track** belongs in an [issue][issues]; open-ended
  thinking belongs in a Discussion.

[issues]: https://github.com/vernonkay/ako/issues

When there is a codebase, this file will grow a build-and-test section. Until then there
is nothing to build.

## Conventions

- **British English**, and no emojis in docs or (later) code.
- **Conventional Commits** for messages (`feat:`, `fix:`, `chore:`, `docs:`, and so on).
- **Stay in the stated scope** of a change; put anything else in an issue.
- **Commas over em dashes**, and headings instead of horizontal rules, to keep the prose
  plain.
- Update docs in the **same** change as the thing they describe.

## Attribution policy

ako is developed with heavy use of AI assistance, disclosed once in the README rather
than per commit. Please **do not add `Co-Authored-By` AI trailers or "Generated with ..."
lines** to commits or pull requests. A commit-message hook and a CI check enforce this
(see [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md)).

that point.
